Hello, I'm an open-source contributor at the **[Open Source Geospatial Foundation (OSGeo)](https://www.osgeo.org/)**, where I participated in Google Summer of Code 2025. My work focused on developing tools to make geospatial datasets more AI-ready using open standards such as GeoCroissant and the SpatioTemporal Asset Catalog (STAC). I recently earned my B.Tech degree in Electronics and Telecommunication from **KDK College of Engineering**.

My interests span across machine learning, deep learning, computer vision, Remote Sensing, and Geospatial AI. Right now, I'm especially interested in data-centric AI (DCAI), Earth Observation (EO), and applying AI techniques to solve challenges across different domains.

Beyond development, I enjoy playing football and reading novels.
